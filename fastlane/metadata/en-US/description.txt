Your library is mostly things you meant to keep — and a long tail of things you never looked at again. Album Sweeper finds that tail and helps you clear it in as few taps as possible.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
There is no account, no server, and no network code in this app at all. Fingerprints are computed on device and screenshots are read by Apple's own Vision framework on the Neural Engine. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nothing to upload to.

THREE KINDS OF DUPLICATE, TREATED DIFFERENTLY
• Exact duplicates — the same picture stored more than once. Identical pixels, dimensions and file size. Nothing to decide, so they arrive already ticked.
• Versions of one photo — a resized or re-compressed copy. One is sharper or larger, so you see them full size and choose. You can keep both.
• Same moment — a burst, or several shots seconds apart. These are different photographs; only you know which one is the good one.

Only the first kind is ever cleared without you looking, and the app shows you why it is safe.

SWIPE THROUGH THE CLUTTER
Chat screenshots, game captures, receipts, maps, system screens. Swipe up to delete, left to keep for now, right to keep forever, down to undo. Every card tells you why it was surfaced, so you are not re-deciding from scratch each time.

IT LEARNS WHAT YOU DELETE
Screenshots from the same app look nearly identical, so they group on their own. Name a group once and hundreds of photos are sorted — and the on-device classifier learns from it. It only ever changes what you are shown first. It never deletes anything.

NOTHING DISAPPEARS BEHIND YOUR BACK
Swiping only writes a note to a list. You review that list, then confirm. iOS then asks you once more, and the photos sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days after that.

Requires access to your photo library. Space is only reclaimed once Recently Deleted expires or you empty it.
